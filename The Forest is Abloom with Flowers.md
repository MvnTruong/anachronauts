[[Evelyn]]’s chapter; the last chapter of book 1

“Mummy look, an Alligator.” the little girl pointing at the creature hidding between the shrubs. She apparently studied before coming here. “That’s not real my, it’s just a statue” she adds, but noticing the look of disappointment growing, she quickly adds “But here is one right here. ‘Hum!’” as her hand chomps down on the girl’s stomach, turning disappointed into giggles.

Elsewhere, a man is proposing to his spouse under the magnolias in full bloom. Applause and whistles following the delayed and nervous ’thank you… I… I mean yes, of course!,

“This is all you’re doing you know,”

Evelyn is taking it all in. “Yes- I mean thank you. of course, thank you. I couldn’t have done it without you Captain.”

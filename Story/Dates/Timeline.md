## Note on what is flooded

At three to four metres it is not true that most of the world is under water. It is about a tenth of the world's people and a small fraction of its land. What is lost is the ports, the deltas, the capitals and most of the places where the power grid meets the coast. Most of what mattered flooded, not most of the world.

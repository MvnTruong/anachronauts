Nations decide to collectively fund deep space missions. Since [[12,140 HE]], every 5 years, a convoy of ships, carry a crew of about 100 or so well be sent out directed at various star systems. Due to the long distance nature, crews are expected to regard this as a one-way mission. Some families are permitted, but most of the crew are single. Your convoy is usually who you will mingle with, though, future ships may travel faster than earlier generations, so there is a chance of ships might catch up to you and transfers may be permitted as requested and/or as necessary.

It’s [[12,157 HE]], Marcus and his crew are onboard the [[Meridica]]. The convoy pick up an [[The Anomaly|anomaly]] and are asked to investigate. [[The Commodore]] tells their team of top particle physicist, led by [[Higbert]] and asked to investigate.

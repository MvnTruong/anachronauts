One of the first [[Chrononauts]] ships. Explorer class ship, 100 capacity.

The Meridica's AI is an [[Ascentients|artificial sentient]] is a tripartite mind structure called and [[Mirella]], [[Derick]] and Rica ([[Rydica]]). There are more minds in the structure, but this these are the dominant ones and they chose their names after the ship. Mirella is the prime operator and is in control of the day to day logistics of the ship and day to day responsibility. Derick is control of analysis, weapons systems, risk management, and life support. Rica is the arbitrator between decisions between Mirella and Derick and is responsible for the well being of the citizens onboard the ship.

Command ship part of the [[Convoy 216|216th deep-space convoy]].

Markers left at [[The Anomaly]] by convoys that pass it between 12,160 and 12,220 HE.

They start as memorials. [[Convoy 216]]'s command ship was lost there with a hundred people aboard and no wreckage, and every convoy that passes leaves something.

They are also research. The physicists work out that the site is a gravity well, that anything inside it is time-dilated rather than destroyed, and roughly when the [[Meridica]] would come out if she survived. Instruments are left alongside the markers as part of a long attempt to understand the structure and why it is asymmetric.

## The recall

Around 12,214 HE, Earth acts on that calculation and sends a package to the site: supplies, an engine capable of about 0.7c, and [[Matteo]] to wait with it.

Recalling the crew on their own 0.1c engine would have cost them another lifetime. Earth sends one that will not.

This is a civilisation in the middle of the Drowning choosing to retrieve a hundred people it had legally written off, decided by a generation that never met them. It is the last time Earth treats them as crew rather than as a caseload. Once they are home they get quarantine, and then a flat.

When [[Marcus]] and the crew come out, they read their own memorials before they find the engine.

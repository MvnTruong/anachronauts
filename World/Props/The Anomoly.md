# Added notes: how it works

Everything above is untouched. This section is an added proposal.

0.7 light years from [[Earth]]. Fired by the [[Kovhal Empire]] in 12,157 HE and failed. What was left was a collapsing remnant with an enormous gravitational field and no event horizon.

## Why it moves them through time

Clocks run slow deep inside a gravity well. The Meridica falls deep. Minutes pass aboard while 64 years pass outside. This is ordinary gravitational time dilation, not travel.

Two things have to be true for the crew to survive:

- **No event horizon.** They have to be able to come back out, so it cannot be a black hole they fall into. A failed throat is the right kind of object.
- **Very large mass.** Tidal forces get gentler as mass goes up. A small compact object would tear the ship apart. This one is big enough that the crew live.

## How they get out

Not by power. You cannot out-burn a well that deep.

They get out on a trajectory. A path that stays unbound falls in and climbs back out by itself, trading speed for height and back again. What kills you is being captured, or crossing a horizon. So survival is a navigation problem with a window measured in seconds, through an object that is not symmetrical.

The throat is collapsing, and that is what opens the window at all. The [[Meridica]]'s minds are what get the ship through it:

- [[Derick]] computes the geometry.
- [[Mirella]] flies it.
- [[Rydica]] rules on whether the loading is survivable.

Marcus's order to divert all power to the engines was the wrong answer and would have killed them. Mirella declines it and does not tell him why. See [[Pull from the Void]].

## The asymmetry

The anomaly is not symmetrical. That is why an escape trajectory exists at all, and it is the thing later researchers are trying to understand when they leave instruments with the [[Totems of Sorrow]].
